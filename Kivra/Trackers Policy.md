About tracking technology
-------------------------

Kivra’s websites and apps use cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies (“tracking technologies”) that involve storing data on, or retrieving data from, your mobile phone, computer, or similar devices. Kivra uses tracking technologies for two purposes: - Necessary tracking technologies: technologies required for Kivra’s services to function. These cannot be opted out of. - Optional tracking technologies: technologies used for analytics or personalization. These require your consent.

Here, you will find information about why Kivra uses tracking technologies, what this means for you, and the choices available to you.

**Your rights**
---------------

Under the Electronic Communications Act, you have the right to be informed about which tracking technologies are used on a website you visit or in an app you use. You also have the right to know how and why tracking technology is used. The provider of a website or app must obtain your consent for any tracking technology that is not strictly necessary. If the use of a tracking technology involves the collection of your personal data, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also applies. You can read more about how Kivra processes your personal data in our [data protection information](https://kivra.se/sv/privat/dataskyddsinformation). Kivra fulfills its obligations under the Electronic Communications Act and the GDPR by: - Informing you that tracking technology is used when you visit our websites or use one of our apps. - Allowing you to choose whether to consent to our use of tracking technology that is not necessary for providing the service you intend to use. - Providing you with this information.

If you would like to learn more about tracking technology in general, and your rights regarding the use of such technology, you can find further information from the [Swedish Post and Telecom Authority](https://www.pts.se/om-oss/behandling-av-personuppgifter/) (PTS) and the [Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).](https://www.imy.se/privatperson/dataskydd/informationssakerhet/sa-surfar-du-mer-anonymt/)

**Your choices**
----------------

#### **In Kivra's apps**

You cannot opt ​​out of essential features because they are necessary for us to provide the services in our apps.

If you do not want Kivra to use tracking technology for other purposes, you can either opt out of it the first time you log in to the app, or later withdraw your consent via your settings in the app. Kivra will then stop collecting data for these purposes, and will delete data we have collected for this purpose within 6 months. If you withdraw your consent, this will not affect the lawfulness of previous processing.

Your consent is linked to you as a user and applies to all devices you log in to.

To change your settings:

*   iOS: Go to More → Settings → Tracking Technologies
    
*   Android: Go to More → Settings → Tracking Technologies
    

#### **On Kivra's websites**

You can turn off and receive warnings about tracking technology in your browser by changing your browser settings. You can also delete previously stored tracking technology through your browser. On Kivra's career page, you make your choices in the page's cookie box. Please note that if you choose to turn off necessary tracking technology, parts of Kivra's services may stop working or be impaired.
