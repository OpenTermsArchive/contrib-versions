### **Cookie Introduction**

  
This cookie policy applies to anyone who visits our website. It explains what cookies are, how we collect data from you, how we use that information and how you can control your cookies.

  

### **What are Cookies?**

  
Like many companies, we use cookies, pixels and other similar technology on our website. Cookies are small text files which are stored on your computer or other devices by your web browser. Cookies can be used to recognise repeat users, language preference, store registration data, login information or facilitate access to and use of the website. Using cookies on our website allows us to understand how you use the site, so we can improve your experience through layout, navigation, content, and, where you have provided consent, personalised advertising.

**Why do we use them?**

We use cookies, pixels, other similar technologies to help us operate our website, personalise content and advertising, provide social media features and to analyse site traffic. We also share information about your use of our site with our social media, advertising and analytics partners. Cookies may be set directly by us (first-party cookies) or by trusted third parties whose services we use on our website (third-party cookies). 

###   

**Types of cookie**

Cookies can be either temporary (session cookie) or permanent (persistent cookie).

**Session cookies** are temporary cookies that remain active while you browse a website. These are usually deleted when you close the browser and therefore only remain active for the duration of your visit.

**Persistent cookies** remain stored on your device, persisting from session to session until you delete them or they reach a set expiration date. Persistent cookies can store information such as encrypted log-in details, bookmarks, and preferred settings and themes - resulting in a faster and smoother web journey.

  

**How to control cookies**

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner. If you'd like to accept all optional cookies, select “Accept all”, if you only wish to allow strictly necessary cookies, select “Reject all” or else you can choose which types you'd like to accept in “Learn more and manage” located on the cookie banner. You can change your preferences at any time by selecting the “Cookie settings” at the bottom of this page which will open the cookie settings screen.

Strictly necessary cookies are always active because they are required for the website to operate. All other cookie categories are optional and will only be used where you have provided consent.

Please note that blocking some types of cookies may impact your experience of the site and the services we are able to offer.
