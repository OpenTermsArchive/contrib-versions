What Are Casinos Not on GamStop?
--------------------------------

GAMSTOP is the free national self-exclusion service for online gambling in Great Britain. When you register, every operator licensed by the UK Gambling Commission must block your access for the period you choose: six months, one year or five years. Casinos not on GamStop simply are not part of that scheme. In practice, that means they are not licensed by the UK Gambling Commission, because the licence conditions require participation.

The label describes what a site is not. It does not tell you what it is. A non GamStop casino might be well run, with a clear ownership structure and a reputable overseas licence, or it might be a thinly documented brand with a licence badge in the footer that proves nothing. The phrase “non GamStop casino” is a category, not a quality mark.

### Non-GamStop, offshore and no-KYC are different things

*   **Non-GamStop** means the operator is not connected to the GAMSTOP register.
*   **Offshore** means the operator is licensed and based outside Great Britain. Most non GamStop casinos UK readers find are offshore, but the two words are not interchangeable.
*   **No-KYC** is a marketing claim that identity checks will not be required. It is not a regulatory category, and it should never be read as a promise.

Another distinction is between accepting British customers and being authorised to serve them. A site may accept sign-ups from the UK, take pounds and display British English without holding permission from the UK Gambling Commission. Currency, language and accessibility are not evidence of authorisation.

Finally, Great Britain and the United Kingdom are not the same for gambling law. The UK Gambling Commission regulates Great Britain, while Northern Ireland has its own arrangements. GAMSTOP is a Great Britain scheme, so readers in Northern Ireland should check how it applies to them.
