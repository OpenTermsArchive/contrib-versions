What GAMSTOP is and why these sites sit outside it
--------------------------------------------------

GAMSTOP is the UK's free self-exclusion scheme. Once you register, every operator holding a UK Gambling Commission licence must block your account for the period you selected, from six months up to a permanent exclusion. Casinos not on GamStop hold licences issued elsewhere, most commonly in Curaçao, so they have no obligation to connect to the UK scheme. That is a licensing difference rather than a way around any law — it simply means a different regulator sits behind the site, with its own rules on disputes, fund protection and advertising standards.

How the money works: paying in, cashing out and costs
-----------------------------------------------------

Costs to the player come mainly through bonus terms rather than entry fees — there is no charge to open an account. What matters is how deposits, withdrawals and wagering interact:

| Term | What it means | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wagering requirement | How many times you must stake the bonus (or deposit plus bonus) before withdrawing winnings | Whether it applies to bonus funds only or your deposit too |
| Game contribution | The percentage each game type counts toward wagering | Slots usually contribute fully; table and live games often less, sometimes zero |
| Maximum bet while wagering | A stake cap enforced until wagering clears | Breaching it can void the bonus balance entirely |
| Maximum conversion | The most bonus-derived winnings you can withdraw | A large headline offer can still be capped low in practice |
| Withdrawal method | Funds are usually returned via the method used to deposit | Processing windows quoted by an operator are targets, not guarantees |

Payment methods vary by operator, but expect a mix of debit cards, e-wallets and, on several non GamStop sites, cryptocurrency options that UK-licensed casinos rarely offer. Always confirm accepted methods and any fees on the operator's own payments page before depositing.
