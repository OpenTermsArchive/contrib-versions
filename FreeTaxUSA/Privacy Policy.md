How we use terms in this policy
-------------------------------

In this policy, "personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with you or your household. Where the law requires us to describe your rights and our practices, we use "personal information" in this sense. Within that broad category, we use two narrower terms for clarity:

*   "Tax return information" means the information you provide to us, or authorize us to obtain, to prepare and file your tax returns (for example, your Social Security number, income, and W-2 and 1099 data). Tax return information is one type of personal information and is also protected under federal law.
*   "Browsing activity" - information we collect through "tracking technologies" such as cookies - means device information and online activity and usage information, such as your IP address, device identifiers, and the pages and features you interact with. Browsing activity is also a type of personal information, and it is the information we "share" with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising, which you can opt out of as described below.

Data retention policy
---------------------

All data collected by us will be retained for a maximum period of eight (8) years calculated from October 31st of the IRS tax filing year in which the return is due, plus a short administrative period for scheduled deletion (the "Deletion Date"). However, your online access to the tax application (the screens where you entered your information) is only available for the current tax year and the prior seven (7) tax years. After a tax year is no longer supported in the application, and until the next October 31st, you may still be able to access and download a PDF copy of your completed return, but you will no longer be able to view or edit the original data entered into the application.

Data will be deleted after October 31st of the 8th year following the IRS tax filing year, regardless of the year the return is actually filed.

For example:

*   Data collected for a 2026 tax return (which has an IRS tax filing deadline in 2027) will be retained until October 31, 2035.
*   Data collected for a 2018 tax return (which has an IRS tax filing deadline in 2019) will be retained until October 31, 2027. Even if the return was filed in a later year, such as 2026.

However, files uploaded to "My Tax Files" (if Deluxe is not purchased), may only be accessed for a period of two (2) years from the date of the current year account creation, plus a short administrative period for scheduled deletion.

Document import and storage services are provided solely as a convenience to you and are subject to this data retention policy. You remain responsible for maintaining your own copies of any records you wish to preserve permanently.

This retention policy applies to all data collected for a given tax year regardless of whether an account is active or inactive. All data collected by a third-party service provider is subject to the service provider's data retention policy, consistent with our contractual requirements and applicable law.

Retargeting and personalized advertising
----------------------------------------

We work with third-party advertising partners to show ads for our products and services across the internet. These partners use cookies and/or other tracking technologies to gather information about your browsing activity on our website to deliver personalized ads based on your interests. We do not upload customer lists to our third-party advertisers. Under some state privacy laws, providing information about your browsing activity to our advertising partners so they can show you ads on other websites may be considered "sharing" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, even though no tax return information or personally identifiable financial information is involved and no money changes hands. You can opt out of this sharing as described below and in the State Privacy Laws section.

If you complete certain actions on our website, such as making a purchase, creating an account, or signing in to our services, we may work with our advertising partners to stop showing you retargeting ads. These actions indicate that you have already engaged with our services, and we aim to enhance your experience by reducing irrelevant ads.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

Apart from the browsing activity described above, we do not share directly identifying personal information (such as your name or contact details) or tax return information with our advertising partners for retargeting purposes. The data collected is solely used to improve the relevance of our ads.

We do not provide your tax return information to advertising partners for any purpose. Our advertising partners may receive online identifiers such as cookie identifiers, advertising click identifiers, and your IP address. We do not use the contents of your tax return - including income, refund or balance due, filing status, or dependents - to target advertising.

To exercise your California opt-out right, use an opt-out preference signal or our opt-out request above.

Privacy preference signals
--------------------------

Global Privacy Control (GPC). We process opt-out preference signals, including the Global Privacy Control (GPC), in a frictionless manner. When we receive a GPC signal from your browser or device, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sharing of your browsing activity for cross-context behavioral advertising. We apply your opt-out at the browser or device level and to any profile associated with that browser or device, and also to your account when you are signed in or otherwise known to us. Using a GPC signal is free, does not change your experience on our site, and does not require you to create an account or provide any information beyond the signal itself. If you later visit without the signal enabled, we will not treat its absence as permission to resume sharing your browsing activity. When you opt out, we stop sending advertising platforms your browsing activity for that visit. We continue to record that a purchase occurred and its value so we can measure how our advertising performs, and the platform may use that information only on our behalf. Because GPC is set per browser, you will need to turn it on in each browser you use.

How to enable GPC. GPC is available through certain browsers and browser extensions that support it. For instructions, see [globalprivacycontrol.org](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/).

Confirmation. We will show you whether your opt-out has been processed - for example, by indicating that your request has been honored when you visit our site with GPC enabled.

Our website does not respond to traditional browser 'Do Not Track' (DNT) signals. Instead, we honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) opt-out preference signal as described in the sections directly above.

hCaptcha
--------

We use the hCaptcha anti-bot service (hereinafter "hCaptcha") on our website. This service is provided by Intuition Machines, Inc., a Delaware US Corporation ("IMI"). hCaptcha is used to check whether the data entered on our website (such as on a login page or contact form) has been entered by a human or by an automated program. To do this, hCaptcha analyzes the behavior of the website or mobile app visitor based on various characteristics. This analysis starts automatically as soon as the website or mobile app visitor enters a part of the website or app with hCaptcha enabled. For the analysis, hCaptcha evaluates various information (e.g. IP address, how long the visitor has been on the website or app, or mouse movements made by the user). The data collected during the analysis will be forwarded to IMI. hCaptcha analysis in the "invisible mode" may take place completely in the background. Website or app visitors are not advised that such an analysis is taking place if the user is not shown a challenge. IMI acts as a "service provider" for the purposes of State Privacy Acts. For more information about hCaptcha and IMI's privacy policy and terms of use, please visit the following links: [https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy) and [https://www.hcaptcha.com/terms](https://www.hcaptcha.com/terms).
